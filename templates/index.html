Notice movement.

Motus explores visible movement patterns using your device camera and helps organize fall-related screening observations.

Start live observation →

Research prototype only. Not a medical device, emergency monitor, or validated fall-prediction system.

✳

Designed around privacy

Pose processing happens in your browser. This prototype does not upload or save camera frames.

Use only with informed consent. Do not rely on this page to prevent, predict, or respond to a fall.

Safety note This prototype can flag sudden changes in detected body position, but those changes are not proof that a person is about to fall. It can miss events and produce false alerts. It must not be used as an emergency alert system.

04 / HOW IT WORKS

Transparent by design

01

Track landmarks

MediaPipe estimates body landmarks from camera frames. It does not understand health status.

02

Measure changes

Experimental rules look for sudden changes in hip position or torso angle over a short interval.

03

Review carefully

Any event is a prompt to review the recording context—not a diagnosis or prediction that a fall will occur.
